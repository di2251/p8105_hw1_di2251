The `penguins` dataset contains size measurements for adult penguins
observed on islands in the the Palmer Archipelago. It contains 344
penguins and measures 8 different variables.


Each penguin belongs to one of three species (Adelie, Gentoo, or
Chinstrap) and was observed on one of three islands (Biscoe, Dream, or
Torgersen).

The measurements include bill length (mm) and depth (mm), flipper length
(mm), and body mass (g), as well as each penguin’s sex and year of
observation.

The mean works for the numeric variable as well as the logical variable
by returning the proportion of values that are `TRUE`. The mean fails
for the character and factor variables and returns `NA` with a warning
stating that the “argument is not numeric or logical: returning NA.”

``` r
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, is_pos))
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, chr_vec))
```

    ## Warning: NAs introduced by coercion

``` r
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, fac_vec))
```

The logical vector converts cleanly with `TRUE` becoming 1 and `FALSE`
becoming 0. The character vector however, becomes `NA` since there is no
way to quantify or order the items. The factor vector becomes 1, 2, and
3.
